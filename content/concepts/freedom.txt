== summary ==
Everything we publish is free software. The four freedoms are not a licensing detail for us; they are why we publish.

== function ==
When a technical choice is close, we decide it by the four freedoms first, then by what the wider free software movement needs, then by what the technical community has learned.

== practice ==
This page is free too: the JavaScript carries licence labels that GNU LibreJS reads, and the whole site works with scripts turned off.
