== summary ==
Every feature that talks to another machine starts switched off. You turn on the ones you want, one at a time, and the program says what each one sends.

== function ==
Consent gates the action mechanically. Before anything touches credentials, someone else's system, or a person who is not part of the conversation, a gate checks that the consent is really there.

== practice ==
This page follows the same rule: it makes no network requests after it loads, sets no cookies, and the prompt at the bottom runs entirely in your browser.
