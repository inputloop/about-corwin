== summary ==
The lens is a thought experiment we run before handing work to someone: what do I think, what do I imagine they think, how are they shaping my view, and how am I shaping theirs.

== function ==
Holding the four apart catches the moments when we have quietly decided for someone else what they want.

== practice ==
It is slow on purpose. It is most useful exactly when we are in a hurry.

== presentation ==
The window is an orb seen from one side, then the other.
