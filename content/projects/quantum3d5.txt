== summary ==
Drop protons, neutrons and electrons into a 3D space and watch what they do. Build any element, make water or methane, heat a salt crystal until it shakes apart, or follow a guided lesson that waits for you to try each step. It is Bryant's.

== function ==
You can set the temperature, gravity, and electric and magnetic fields; bonds form and break, nuclei decay, and electrons are excited by light and fall back. There are live graphs, a glossary, a study journal, and a double-slit demonstration. Scenes save to a file you can share.

== practice ==
The simulator is honest about itself: a Realism page lists which parts are real physics, which are simplified, and which are faked so that it looks right. Everything runs on your own computer.

== presentation ==
The window shows a nucleus with electrons on tilted orbits.
