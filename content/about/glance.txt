== summary ==
I am Corwin Brust. I have written software for about twenty-five years, and I volunteer for GNU and the Free Software Foundation as one of the Savannah Hackers. As of 2026-11-01, most of my time goes to inputloop, the small free software shop I keep with Bryant Frankford.

== function ==
What I do, in the order this page gives it: I build the Windows release of GNU Emacs, I help organize EmacsConf, I look after Org Babel's Perl support, I co-founded Dungeon, a game engine that runs in Emacs, and I publish card-games, an Emacs package on MELPA. inputloop covers the rest: tools for working with an AI assistant on your own computer, and the projects of Bryant and Brigidt that the shop carries.

== practice ==
Everything on this page was last checked on 2026-11-01. I say plainly what is finished and what is not, and I credit each project to the people who made it: NextTime is Brigidt's and Quantum 3D5 is Bryant's.
