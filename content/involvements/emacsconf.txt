== summary ==
EmacsConf is a yearly online conference about Emacs and the people who use it. It is put together by volunteers and streamed with free software. I am one of the core organizers.

== function ==
Organizing means the unglamorous middle: helping speakers get recordings in, keeping the schedule honest, hosting sessions, and fixing whatever broke five minutes before it airs.

== practice ==
I have also spoken there. In 2020, Trivial Emacs Kits was about giving a newcomer a short init.el they can read, one small file at a time, and about letting a group of friends share defaults so the first hour with Emacs is gentler. The same year, Erik Elmshauser and I presented Dungeon Mode. In 2023 I gave Windows into Freedom, about the Windows port.

== presentation ==
The window is a ring of people taking turns: one speaks, the ring passes the turn along. It is a picture of a conference that runs by handing things to each other.
