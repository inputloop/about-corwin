How this page works

It is one page of HTML and CSS that reads well with scripts turned off. The scripts add the moving windows, the parallax, the cards that grow, and the prompt; GNU LibreJS can check their licence labels. Nothing is fetched after the page loads and nothing is sent about you. Your Customize settings and whether you turned motion off stay in your own browser. The blog posts and build list were saved when the page was built.

Keys
