How this page works

It is one page of HTML and CSS that reads well with scripts turned off. The scripts add the moving windows, the parallax, the cards that grow, and the prompt; GNU LibreJS can check their licence labels. Nothing is fetched after the page loads and nothing is stored about you, except whether you turned motion off, which stays in your own browser.

Keys

  • Alt+x (as M-x in Emacs): go to the prompt. There are no single-key shortcuts.
  • Escape: close the open card. In the prompt, Escape or Ctrl+g clears it.
  • ↑ and ↓ in the prompt: earlier and later questions.

With scripts off, every card's introduction, back and scoreboard open under Read about, and the quick filters below still work.
